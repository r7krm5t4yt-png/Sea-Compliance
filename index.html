The Challenge

Around the world, fishing vessels and maritime operators continue to rely on fragmented, paper-based systems to manage compliance, safety, maintenance, and crew records. These disconnected processes increase administrative burden, reduce operational visibility, and make regulatory compliance more difficult. As safety expectations and environmental responsibilities continue to grow, the maritime sector requires digital solutions that improve governance, strengthen collaboration, and support a safe, sustainable, and resilient Blue Economy.

Paper-Based Compliance

Manual documentation creates inefficiencies, duplication, and increased risk of lost or incomplete records.

Safety & Operational Risk

Limited visibility of inspections, maintenance, incidents, and corrective actions can compromise safety performance.

Disconnected Stakeholders

Vessel operators, regulators, auditors, training providers, and insurers often work in isolated systems with limited information sharing.

Limited Digital Adoption

Many small and medium-sized fishing enterprises still lack affordable digital tools designed specifically for their operational and regulatory needs.

Who We Are

Sea Compliance is a maritime technology company dedicated to advancing safety, compliance, and operational excellence across the Blue Economy. We develop intelligent digital solutions that enable fishing companies, vessel operators, regulators, training institutions, and maritime stakeholders to manage compliance more efficiently, reduce operational risk, and make informed decisions through trusted data.

Our integrated platform combines digital safety management, crew competency management, compliance monitoring, audit management, maintenance planning, incident reporting, document control, and business intelligence into a single connected ecosystem. By replacing fragmented paper-based processes with secure digital workflows, Sea Compliance strengthens governance, improves collaboration, and supports safer, more sustainable, and more resilient maritime operations.
