# Sea Compliance

## Digitising Compliance. Strengthening Safety. Enabling the Blue Economy.

Sea Compliance is a maritime technology company developing digital solutions to improve safety management, regulatory compliance, operational efficiency, and sustainability across the Blue Economy.

## About Sea Compliance

Sea Compliance provides digital infrastructure for maritime operators, fishing companies, regulators, auditors, training institutions, and other maritime stakeholders.

Our mission is to transform fragmented compliance processes into connected digital workflows that improve transparency, accountability, and decision-making.

## The Challenge

Many maritime organisations continue to rely on fragmented paper-based systems for managing:

- Safety Management Systems
- Crew certification
- Audits and inspections
- Risk assessments
- Maintenance records
- Regulatory documentation

These processes create administrative challenges and limit operational visibility.

## Our Solution

The Sea Compliance Digital Platform is designed to integrate:

- Digital Safety Management Systems
- Crew Competency Management
- Compliance Monitoring
- Audit Management
- Risk Management
- Document Control
- Operational Analytics

## Supporting the Blue Economy

Sea Compliance contributes to a safer, more sustainable and resilient maritime sector by supporting:

- Improved maritime safety
- Stronger compliance culture
- Digital transformation
- Responsible fisheries
- Better governance
